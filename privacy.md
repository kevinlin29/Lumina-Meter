Privacy Policy — Lumina Meter                                                                                       
   
  Last updated: October 29, 2026
                                                                                                      
  Lumina Meter ("the App") is developed by ARCTILLO LABS. This policy describes how the App handles your information.
                                                                                                                      
  Camera Access
  The App uses your device's camera solely to measure scene luminance and calculate exposure values. All camera data
  is processed locally on your device in real time. No images or video are captured, stored, or transmitted.

  Data Collection
  ARCTILLO LABS does not collect, receive, transmit, or share your personal data or app content. There are no user
  accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud sync. Optional purchases and purchase restoration communicate
  with Apple's App Store as described below.

  Optional Purchases (Lumina 2.2 and later)
  Lumina uses Apple's StoreKit to offer an optional one-time Pro purchase, verify access, and restore purchases.
  Apple processes payment and purchase information under its own privacy policies. ARCTILLO LABS does not operate a
  payment server, receive your payment-card information, or upload your inventory, imported photos, camera frames,
  or exposure records. StoreKit may contact Apple to load products or verify purchases; verified purchase access
  can be read from StoreKit's on-device records. Earlier access is checked using Apple's verified app transaction.

  Local Storage
  The App stores your preferences, film inventory, roll history, camera and lens records, exposure logs, service
  history, notes, gear photos you choose, and film-label photos locally on your device using standard system storage.
  Lumina does not sync this content to a developer or third-party server. Removing the App may remove this locally
  stored content, subject to the backup and restore settings you control in iOS.

  Photo Library
  When you add a gear or film-label photo, Lumina uses Apple's system photo picker. Lumina receives only the image you
  explicitly select and stores a copy in the local collection. The App does not upload the image.

  Data Control and Deletion
  You can create a local backup, export collection data as CSV, or permanently delete film inventory, roll history,
  gear records, photos, labels, service records, and exposure logs from Lumina's Settings screen. Exported files leave
  Lumina only when you choose a destination through an iOS system file picker. You can also remove all locally stored
  app data by deleting the App from your device.

  Third-Party Services
  The App does not integrate with third-party APIs, analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Support and Privacy links
  open public pages in your browser only when you tap them. Lumina does not append collection, meter, or device data to
  those links.

  Children's Privacy
  The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
